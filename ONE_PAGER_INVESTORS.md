# EV Charger Simulator — Investor One Pager
## Infrastructure software for the rapidly scaling EV charging market

### Investment thesis
EV charging networks are expanding quickly, but reliability and interoperability remain major bottlenecks. The EV Charger Simulator addresses this gap by enabling operators and platform teams to test charging behavior at scale before production rollout.

### Problem
EV ecosystem players face expensive, slow, and inconsistent validation cycles:
- Hardware-dependent testing slows product delivery.
- Production incidents from untested edge cases increase downtime and support cost.
- Multi-vendor OCPP integration creates recurring QA complexity.

### Solution
A software-only charger simulation platform that reproduces real station behavior for engineering, QA, and operations:
- Simulates single stations, multi-connector sites, and full fleets.
- Emulates core OCPP 1.6J message flows and remote-control commands.
- Compresses test time with accelerated-session simulation.
- Enables repeatable, automated regression and load testing.

### Strategic value proposition
- **Mission-critical reliability layer:** improves charger uptime and network trust.
- **Cost-efficiency driver:** reduces hardware lab dependency and manual QA effort.
- **Scalable go-to-market enabler:** shortens deployment timelines for growing charge networks.
- **Platform expansion path:** foundation for analytics, compliance validation, and managed test services.

### Market context
- EV adoption and charging infrastructure rollout continue to accelerate globally.
- Operators need dependable tooling to onboard hardware vendors and maintain SLA-grade performance.
- As networks scale, software-led validation becomes non-optional.

### Monetization options
- Annual SaaS licenses by environment and station volume
- Tiered plans for advanced scenarios (fleet stress, diagnostics, reporting)
- Enterprise support and integration packages
- White-label QA modules for platform partners

### Why now
Charging operators are moving from pilot projects to large-scale deployments. The shift creates immediate demand for tools that reduce integration risk, protect uptime, and speed rollout.

### Execution focus
1. Deepen protocol and scenario coverage.
2. Expand automation and reporting for enterprise QA teams.
3. Integrate with CI/CD and observability stacks.
4. Build channel partnerships with EV software and hardware ecosystems.

### Vision
Become the default simulation and validation layer for EV charging software delivery worldwide.
